How it works

Five steps, one consultant

Most of the work is done before you meet a consulate. This is the whole sequence, with nothing hidden behind it.

  1. 01

    Tell us your plan

    Destination, dates, purpose of travel and how many applicants. Five minutes on the form, or a WhatsApp message.

  2. 02

    We map the route

    We confirm which country you must apply through, which VFS centre handles it, and what that authority actually requires.

  3. 03

    Documents reviewed

    You send everything through. A person reads every page and returns a gap list, so nothing is discovered at the counter.

  4. 04

    Appointment and submission

    We book the biometric slot, complete the form and brief you on exactly what to present on the day.

  5. 05

    Decision and travel

    We track the file to the decision, then your consultant stays on call right through to departure.

FAQ

Questions we are asked most

Five of the most common. The rest are on the full FAQ page.

Most applications are decided within 7 to 15 working days of the consulate receiving your file, though seasonal peaks and the country you apply through both affect this. Appointment availability is usually the slowest part of the timeline, which is why we book before anything else is finalised.

You apply through the country where you will spend the longest, and if those are equal, through your first point of entry. Spending two nights in France and eight in Italy means applying through Italy. Tell us your itinerary and we will confirm the correct authority before you pay anything.

The core set is a passport valid at least three months beyond your trip, a completed application form, passport photos, travel insurance covering at least EUR 30,000, proof of accommodation, proof of funds and an itinerary. The exact list shifts between countries and visa types, so we send a checklist specific to your application rather than a generic one.

Almost always, yes. First-time applicants normally give fingerprints and a photograph at a VFS Global or TLScontact centre in the UK. We book the earliest realistic slot and tell you exactly what to bring and how long the appointment takes.

Our service fees start at £80 and are quoted in full before you pay anything. Government visa fees, VFS appointment fees and travel insurance are paid separately and are never marked up. If we cannot deliver the service you bought, our refund policy returns the service fee we did not earn.

Privacy policy

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect the name, email address, telephone number, destination and any travel details you provide. This is used only to answer your enquiry and to prepare your application.

Who we share it with

We share your details only with the authorities and appointment centres required to process your visa, for example VFS Global, TLScontact and the relevant consulate. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry records for as long as needed to support your application and to meet our legal obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email info@travelade.co.uk and we will action it.

Refund policy

If we deliver the service

Our fee covers the work listed in your package. Once that work is done, the fee is non-refundable, because it has been spent on your application.

If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to provide a service you have paid for, for example we cannot find an appointment we promised, or we cancel your package for any reason, we refund the undelivered part of our service fee in full.

If the application is refused

A refusal is a decision made by the consulate, not by us. Where a refusal is caused by an error in a document or form we prepared, we will correct that error and resubmit at no further charge.

Government and appointment fees

Visa fees, VFS appointment fees and insurance premiums paid to third parties cannot be refunded by us, as we do not hold them. Those refunds follow the third party's own policy.
