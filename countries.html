Schengen Countries We Cover

How the Schengen Visa Works for UK Applicants

The Schengen Area covers 29 member states: 27 within the European Union plus Iceland, Norway, Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Ireland and Cyprus are not part of the Schengen visa agreement, so a Schengen visa does not replace an Irish or Cypriot visa. A short-stay Schengen visa issued by any one member state is valid for travel across the whole zone for the period it is granted, so you do not apply separately for each country you plan to visit.

You must apply to the country where you intend to spend the longest stay. If your plans are split evenly across two countries, you apply to the one where you will arrive first. Supporting evidence such as flight bookings and hotel reservations should match the country you nominate.

Applications from UK residents are usually lodged through a visa application centre such as VFS Global or TLScontact, depending on the destination country. You attend in person for biometric enrollment, which is where fingerprints and a compliant photograph are captured. Processing normally takes 15 to 21 calendar days, though consulates may take longer where additional checks are needed.

Schengen Visa Types

Short-Stay Schengen Visa (Type C)

The Type C visa covers tourism, business visits, short-term study and medical treatment, and permits up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen zone. This is the visa we handle most often. See our packages and pricing.

Long-Stay Schengen Visa (Type D)

Type D is for stays exceeding 90 days, such as employment, study or family reunification. It is issued by a single country and does not give you the right to work before the permit is approved. Applications are made directly to the relevant consulate or immigration authority rather than through a visa application centre.

Documents We Prepare For Your Schengen Visa

Every application needs a consistent set of documents, and a single mismatch is the most common reason for refusal. Our team assembles and checks the full file before submission, which covers:

  • Valid passport meeting the 10-year validity rule, issued within the last 10 years and valid 3 months beyond your trip
  • Passport photographs to the specific size and background the destination requires
  • Completed and signed Schengen visa application form
  • Travel medical insurance with minimum €30,000 Schengen-wide cover
  • Confirmed flight booking and accommodation for your full itinerary
  • Proof of funds such as bank statements covering at least three months of activity
  • Employment letter or business registration evidence where applicable
  • Cover letter explaining your travel purpose and ties to the UK

Schengen Visa Fees and Processing Times

The visa fee itself is set by the destination consulate and is paid directly to the visa application centre, so it varies by country and application type. Our service fee is separate and covers appointment booking, document preparation, form completion and submission support. Compare our three packages.

Standard processing is 15 to 21 calendar days from your appointment date. Complex files can take up to 45 days. Booking early is the single most effective way to avoid a rushed application, and consulate processing times vary during peak travel season.
