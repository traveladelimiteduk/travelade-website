Documents Required for a Schengen Visa

Every Schengen visa application from the UK needs the same core set of documents. Requirements vary slightly by destination consulate, so confirm the exact specification for the country you are applying through before you book. Below is the standard checklist.

Valid Passport

Your passport must meet these requirements:

  • Issued within the last 10 years
  • Valid at least 3 months beyond your intended departure from the Schengen area
  • At least 2 blank pages
  • No damage or wear

Application Form

Complete visa application form:

  • Accurate information
  • Signed by applicant
  • Date and sign within 15 days of appointment

Photos

Passport photograph requirements:

  • 2 recent color photos
  • White background
  • 35x45mm size

Flight Itinerary

Travel booking proofs:

  • Round-trip flight booking
  • Can be provisional booking

Accommodation

Proof of lodging:

  • Hotel booking confirmation
  • Host invitation letter (if staying with someone)

Proof of Funds

Financial documents:

  • 3 months bank statements
  • Original bank letter

  • Valid for all Schengen countries
  • Cover entire stay

Cover Letter

Purpose of travel:

  • Explain travel purpose
  • Itinerary details

How to Check Your Documents Before You Apply

Most refusals are caused by documents that contradict each other rather than by anything unusual in your circumstances. Before your appointment, lay every document out on one surface and check that the dates line up: your flight booking should fall inside your insurance dates, your hotel booking should match your travel dates, and your employment letter should be dated recently.

Check that the name on your passport, your application form, your bank statements and your insurance policy is spelled identically. A middle name included on one document but omitted from another is a routine cause of an application being held for further checks.

Which Country Are You Applying Through?

You must apply to the Schengen country where you plan to spend the longest stay, or the one where you arrive first if your trip is split evenly. Each consulate sets its own appointment centre, photograph standard and fee, so the country you nominate affects your whole checklist.
