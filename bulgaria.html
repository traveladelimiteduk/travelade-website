FAQ

Bulgaria visa questions, answered straight

The questions we are asked most about applying through Bulgaria from the UK.

You apply through the country where you spend the most nights, and if those are equal, through your first point of entry. Spending two nights in Bulgaria and eight in Italy means applying through Italy. Tell us your itinerary and we confirm the correct authority before you pay anything.

Most applications are decided within 15 calendar days of the consulate receiving your file, and many are decided faster. Complex files can take up to 45 days. Appointment availability is usually the slowest part of the timeline, which is why we book before anything else is finalised.

Bulgaria applications from the UK are lodged at the official Bulgaria visa application centre in the UK, where biometrics are taken for first-time applicants. We book the slot and tell you exactly what to bring and how long the appointment takes.

The government visa fee and appointment-centre charges are paid by you, direct to the authorities, and are never marked up. Your package covers the consultancy work around the application, quoted in full before you pay anything.

A refusal is a decision made by the consulate, not by us. Where a refusal is caused by an error in a document or form we prepared, we correct that error and resubmit at no further charge. Premium package files also get resubmission support if the consulate asks for more.
